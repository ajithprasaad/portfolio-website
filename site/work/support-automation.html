Business Problem

Support team couldn't keep up with policy questions. Documentation was scattered across multiple systems, and approximately 40% of tickets required escalation to engineers. That created bottlenecks and slowed response times.

My Approach

Designed and built a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline over the policy corpus. Used LangChain for orchestration, OpenAI APIs for generation, and pgvector for semantic search. Implemented guardrails to prevent hallucination and LLM-as-judge evals to measure answer quality.
