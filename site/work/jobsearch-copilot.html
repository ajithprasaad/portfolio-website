02Problem

The list got so long it became THE JOB.

Follow-ups hid. Warm and cold trails blurred. Status and filters fought the seeker more than they helped.

03Design decisions
01

7-state pipeline

Status started as free text. It drifted. A closed set of 7 (Saved, Applied, Phone Screen, Interview, Offer, Reject, Withdrawn) covers nearly all applications. Each state maps to a concrete next action, not just a label.

02

Priority as derived rank

Computed from funnel depth, due proximity, and recency. Not manually tagged. Warm stages surface; cold trails sink.

03

Notes layered into a drawer

Inline notes created clutter. A drawer keeps the table scannable. Detail on demand with one extra click, removes visual noise.

The queue stays deliberately minimal so it never competes with the search.

04What I would test next

Next: Let a fourteen-day cold window auto-prune the queue. Also, a calendar reminder for pending tasks would be very useful.

Validation comes down to adoption. I find myself using the spreadsheet less and less, and the tool really helps bring more focus into my job search.

05Stack

The hardest cut was scope. Keeping it to a single surface forced every feature to earn its place.
